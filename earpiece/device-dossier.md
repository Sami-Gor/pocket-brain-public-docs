# Earpiece — device dossier

## Product intent

Earpiece is an open-ear audio and voice concept within the Pocket Brain device ecosystem. Its intended acoustic arrangement directs sound toward the ear without sealing the ear canal. Acoustic quality, voice performance and physical wearability are not validated product claims.

## Concept presentation

The V2.2 assembly shows a front pod, rear pod and connecting hook. The front pod depicts audio and sensing elements. The rear pod depicts electronics, power and connectivity allocation. The hook depicts support and connecting elements.

The presentation maps 59 V2.2 component identities. These identify model elements for inspection; they do not prove functioning electronics or selected, qualified production parts.

## Engineering relationship

Current engineering belongs to the separate v3 program. Its authority is under reconciliation, and the historical V2.2 presentation must not be used as a manufacturing representation of v3.

## Qualification limits

Physical materials, supplier parts, battery capacity, charging performance, RF, acoustics, thermal behavior and physical validation remain incomplete or unestablished in this snapshot. Fabrication is NOT READY.
