# Eyewear — mechanical architecture

> **Public snapshot · 2026-10-03.** The private Pocket Brain engineering repository remains the source authority. This curated public summary is not a fabrication release, validated product specification or distribution of engineering source files.

## Assembly intent

The frame carries right-front optical structures and asymmetric temple assemblies. Digital modelling includes retention, hinge, acoustic, thermal and service relationships. The optical assembly includes calibrated relationships that cannot be treated as freely interchangeable manufactured parts.

## Authority and evidence limits

The V2.0.1 web assembly is the presentation authority for its semantic mapping and existing interactions. S24 / v0.14 is a separate reported mechanical track. Its source cache does not substitute for independently recovered authoritative exports, sign-off sweeps or physical validation.

The reported 80° nesting fold has not been independently reproduced. The default viewer's 95° hinge movement does not certify that fold. A historical v0.8 export with failed 85° checks does not disprove or verify the newer report.

Blender-source electronics placement supports only V2.0.1 reserved pockets. The separate runtime electronics layer has a placement/export-transform mismatch (**EW-E2 OPEN**). Fit in v0.14 remains unverified. The historical electronics GLB relationship remains **NOT_VERIFIABLE**.

## Manufacturing and service boundary

Physical fit, hinge strength, sealing, thermal behaviour, assembly repeatability and structural validation remain open. Digital service/movement records do not prove physical service access. Material grades, suppliers and manufacturing tolerances require confirmation. Fabrication remains **NOT READY**.
