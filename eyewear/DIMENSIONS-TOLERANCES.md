# Eyewear — dimensions & tolerances

> **Public snapshot · 2026-10-03.** The private Pocket Brain engineering repository remains the source authority. This curated public summary is not a fabrication release, validated product specification or distribution of engineering source files.

## Reported S24 / v0.14 envelope

| Item | Reported value | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Deployed envelope | 160.4 × 144.8 × 55.2 mm | Reported only; authoritative export recovery pending |
| Stored envelope | 154.2 × 39.3 × 55.2 mm at 80° | Reported fold not independently reproduced |
| Temple wall | 1.25 mm | Provisional report; not a manufacturing tolerance |

No production tolerance, supplier acceptance, GD&T, fit clearance or prototype sign-off is established by these values. Modelled component envelopes do not validate physical packaging.

The reported 67 CAD bodies, 195 independently audited solids and six V2.1 presentation objects describe different representations. They are not interchangeable part counts. A historical CAD calculation of 574.6 g used default densities and is not measured physical mass.

**Fabrication remains NOT READY.** Material grades, process capability, thermal expansion, hinge repeatability, optical alignment, sealing and structural validation remain open. S24 / v0.14 remains **SOURCE_EXISTS_BUT_NOT_EXPORTED**.
