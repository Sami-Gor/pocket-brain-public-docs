Pocket Brain Ring is an intended finger-worn interaction accessory with motion sensing, haptic feedback and BLE connectivity. Its digital assembly is inspectable, but no functioning physical prototype is validated.

**Fabrication: NOT READY.** Mechanical production authority remains Fusion v0.4. M3 adoption is unresolved; schematic reconciliation, connectivity, routing, RF and power work remain open.
