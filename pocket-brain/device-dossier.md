This curated summary is not the fabrication or engineering authority. Raw engineering source remains private. Underlying engineering source retained in private source repository.

## Intended role

Pocket Brain is a screenless local compute core, with compute, memory, storage, battery/power, thermal management and I/O provisions. Ecosystem pairing is product intent, not a verified end-to-end hardware demonstration.

## What is not established

Fabrication readiness, supplier-qualified battery capacity/runtime, charging safety, RF performance, thermal performance, sealing, material grades, production tolerances and physical prototype qualification remain open. Render materials and semantic labels cannot certify these properties.

The **37 semantic components**, **18 CAD components** and **443 CAD bodies** are different representations, not competing counts of physical electronic parts. See [system architecture]({{ '/pocket-brain/system-architecture/' | relative_url }}).
