> Device: Pocket Brain

This is not the fabrication or engineering authority. Raw engineering source remains private. Underlying engineering source retained in private source repository.

## Nominal frozen architecture

Fusion v0.3 is the mechanical authority. The enclosure caps, internal frame, battery support, compute-board carrier, I/O cassette, port provision and ventilation retention form the nominal mechanical assembly. Gate D is closed geometrically; older unresolved vent-retention experiments are historical, not the current architecture.

The reconciled record reports 240/240 vent tubes accounted for, with zero missing, duplicate or free-escape cases. Assembly replay passes 12 steps with zero impossible insertions or deadlocks. Native interference classification reports zero invalid and zero unclassified contacts among 814 classified interferences.

## Qualification boundary

These are nominal geometric results. Supplier tolerances, press-fit forces, adhesive/bond specification, material/manufacturing-process qualification and prototype testing remain open. A nominal press/contact does not prove a manufacturable fit, adhesive life or load capacity.

The thermal bridge remains a reference-only provision with an open fit/performance issue. Electrical component fit and reset/USB accommodation decisions remain open despite the mechanical freeze.

Blender v1.0 accounts for all 443 Fusion solids, but its presentation overlays do not authorize changes to the frozen mechanical source. No CAD, mesh, drawing or fabrication download is provided by this snapshot.
